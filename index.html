Areas of expertise.

A sample of where we add the most value — from "will anyone buy this?" to a confident launch, and the system your team runs on afterward. Bring us in for one piece, the whole path, or a problem that sits somewhere in between.

01 — Price it

Conjoint analysis

The question it answers

Which features matter, which don’t, and what will each customer segment pay before demand drops off?

What you walk away with

Survey design and fielding, feature-importance and willingness-to-pay by segment, and a price–volume simulator your team can keep using.

02 — Prove it

Techno-economic analysis

The question it answers

If the process works at bench scale, does it still pay off at commercial scale — and against which incumbents?

What you walk away with

A transparent cost model (capex, opex, inputs), competitor and market review, and sensitivity analysis showing which assumptions make or break the business case.

03 — Pick it

Portfolio selection

The question it answers

You have more promising projects than money. Which ones deserve the next dollar?

What you walk away with

Scoring criteria tied to your strategy, a stage-gate screen applied consistently across every candidate, and a ranked shortlist with the reasoning written down.

04 — Charter it

Project chartering

The question it answers

Do the sponsor, the team and the budget holder agree on scope, success measures and resources before work starts?

What you walk away with

A signed charter: objectives, scope boundaries, success metrics, budget, roles, key risks and decision rights — built with your sponsor, not handed to them.

05 — Deliver it

Project & program management

The question it answers

Now that it’s funded, who keeps the work, the vendors and the stakeholders moving in the same direction?

What you walk away with

An integrated plan, a steady reporting cadence, active risk and issue management, and stage-gate readiness reviews through to launch.

06 — Sell it

Launch & sales materials

The question it answers

Your buyer is a hospital administrator, a distributor or an agronomist. What evidence and economics will get them to yes?

What you walk away with

Buyer-facing value dossiers and sales collateral: the product story, clinical or field evidence, pricing and purchase options, reimbursement guidance and ROI scenarios built around the customer’s own numbers.

07 — Run it

Airtable setup & automations

The question it answers

Where do your projects, portfolio scores and status updates actually live — and how much of it is copied by hand between spreadsheets, email and Slack?

What you walk away with

An Airtable base designed around your workflow — project and portfolio trackers, intake forms, interfaces and dashboards — plus automations for status updates, approvals and notifications, and a handoff your team can maintain.
